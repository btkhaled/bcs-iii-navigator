7 — Takeaway

Mechanism first.
Quantification second.
Uncertainty always explicit.

The framework separates literature-derived inputs, calculated quantities, assumptions and model predictions. Within the modeled formulation space, conventional excipient combinations increase predicted F from 1.38% to 18.62%. The next step is to quantify parameter and structural uncertainty and identify which experimental measurements would most reduce predictive uncertainty.
